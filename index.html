I am currently a JSPS Fellow at Waseda University, where I am visiting until June 2025.

I am also a postdoctoral researcher working at Universität Hamburg, in the Collaborative Research Center "Higher Structures, Moduli Spaces and Integrability." I gained my PhD in 2024 from the University of Edinburgh, under the supervision of David Jordan and Pavel Safronov.

I am interested in topological field theories: their defects, associated moduli spaces, and approaches via skein theory and factorization homology.
